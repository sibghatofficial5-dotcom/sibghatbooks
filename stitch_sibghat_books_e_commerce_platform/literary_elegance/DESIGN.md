## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the tactile dignity, quiet composure, and bespoke heritage of a high-end literary press and independent book salon. Drawing inspiration from classical typography, editorial publication standards, and curated physical spaces like Daunt Books and Persephone Books, the visual language respects literature as a permanent artifact rather than disposable media.

The target audience comprises bibliophiles, discerning collectors, literary critics, and design-conscious readers who value provenance, material weight, and thoughtful curation. The interface creates an atmosphere of contemplative stillness—a calm, intellectual haven isolated from digital hyper-stimulation.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Editorial Modernism** with **Tactile Heritage**:
- **Generous Spatial Rhythms**: Whitespace functions like wide book margins, providing breathing room for cover art and long-form prose.
- **Architectural Framing**: Restrained, hair-thin divider rules evoke printed colophons, chapter breaks, and tipped-in frontispieces.
- **Subtle Physicality**: Flat digital layers are enriched with delicate, book-spine dimensionality and paper-stock tone tiers instead of synthetic glows or neon highlights.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical archival materials: aged acid-free paper, rich bookbinder cloth, cold-pressed ink, and hand-stamped gold foil leaf.

- **Primary (`#1E352B`)**: Deep Evergreen. Acts as the primary anchor for branded elements, prominent calls to action, active navigation markers, and header ornaments.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted Archival Gold / Brass. Used with deliberate parsimony for insignia, foil-stamped award medallions, monograms, and subtle rule flourishes.
- **Tertiary (`#2D4C3E`)**: Woodland Moss. Functions as a transitional accent for secondary states, hover highlights on dark surfaces, and editorial badge fills.
- **Neutral Surface Palette**:
  - `Canvas Canvas / Ground`: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Bone/Ivory), establishing the base paper tone across all viewports.
  - `Surface Low / Container`: `#F5F0E8` (Pressed Cream), used for shelving tiers, card enclosures, and modal containers.
  - `Surface Highlight`: `#ECE4D6` (Linen Parchment), applied to borders, structural lines, and inset compartments.
- **Text & Ink Palette**:
  - `Primary Ink`: `#191817` (Lampblack Ink), strictly enforced across all literary titles and primary headings.
  - `Secondary Ink`: `#2D2B28` (Charcoal Carbon), used for body text and long-form reviews to ensure high legibility with zero glare.
  - `Muted Ink`: `#5A5650` (Pencil Umber), reserved for publication meta, ISBN numbers, copyright notations, and captions.

## Typography

The typography couples the humanistic, scholarly proportions of **EB Garamond** with the clear, geometric precision of **DM Sans**.

- **Editorial Hierarchy**: Display and headline sizes employ `EB Garamond` in regular and medium weights. Large headlines emulate classic title pages, set with slightly tightened tracking and generous line spacing to prevent ascending and descending collisions.
- **Reading Comfort**: Long-form excerpts, synopsis copy, and curator notes use `EB Garamond` at `17px` to `20px` with a generous `1.6x` line-height ratio, recreating the optical ease of reading a handset letterpress volume.
- **Modern Utility**: System navigations, numeric prices, inventory stamps, filter metadata, and functional form elements switch cleanly to `DM Sans`. All uppercase labels (e.g., `label-sm`, `label-md`) utilize wide letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to maintain legibility at compact dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric, fixed-boundary grid system modeled on classic book layouts (such as the Van de Graaf canon and Tschichold’s golden section principles).

- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width 1360px)**: Uses `margin: 4rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Book showcases feature deliberate spatial friction: wide text columns paired with generous whitespace channels rather than edge-to-edge density.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Uses `margin-tablet: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Editorial sidebars shift smoothly into collapsible top modules.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Uses `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Spacing preserves an uncluttered reading cadence with minimal chrome.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Section boundaries prioritize vertical separation over horizontal dividers, utilizing `space-xl` (48px) and multiples of 24px/32px to let book cover art dominate the visual plane.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid the artificial appearance of heavy drop shadows or glassy blurs, this design system creates depth through **tonal paper stepping**, **hairline borders**, and **tangible book-spine dimensional shading**:

1. **Surface Tiers**:
   - Level 0 (Base Ground): `#FDFBF7`
   - Level 1 (Shelves & Section Modules): `#F5F0E8`
   - Level 2 (Curated Cards & Flyout Drawers): `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#FDFBF7` bordered by `#ECE4D6`

2. **Hairline Outlines**:
   Instead of thick borders or harsh dividers, components employ a subtle 1px border colored with `#ECE4D6` (on light ground) or translucent dark charcoal (`rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.08)`).

3. **Spine & Jacket Shadows**:
   Book covers incorporate an asymmetric multi-stop shadow to simulate physical thickness, cloth grain, and page block weight:
   - Base edge: `0 1px 2px rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.05)`
   - Spine cast: `-2px 0 4px rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.06)`
   - Surface elevation: `0 8px 24px rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.04)`

4. **Hover Transitions**:
   On hover, interactive book jackets rise subtly (`transform: translateY(-4px)`) while expanding the ambient shadow softly to `0 16px 36px rgba(25, 24, 23, 0.12)`, preserving a tactile, physics-based response.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language, reflecting the clean geometry of book jackets, letterpress galleys, trimmed paper stocks, and traditional cloth bindings.

- **Buttons, inputs, image frames, and cards**: Maintain crisp `0px` radii, conveying precision, stability, and enduring craftsmanship.
- **Circular Elements**: Pure circles (`border-radius: 50%`) are reserved exclusively for edition seals, author cameo avatars, and pagination dots to contrast purposefully with the rectangular architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Evergreen background (`#1E352B`), Warm Ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), sharp rectangular corners, uppercase label typography (`label-md`), padded at `14px 28px`. Hover state: smooth shift to Tertiary Woodland Moss (`#2D4C3E`).
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1px solid Deep Charcoal (`#191817`) border, transparent fill, Deep Charcoal text. Hover state: background transitions to `#ECE4D6` with zero border movement.
- **Tertiary / Link**: EB Garamond italicized or DM Sans uppercase with a 1px border-bottom underline resting 3px below the text baseline. Hover state: underline expands or transitions to Archival Gold (`#C5A059`).

### Book Cards & Jacket Display
- Constructed on a 0px container with a `#F5F0E8` base tone.
- Front covers feature an inset 1px inner border simulating book cloth debossing.
- The spine side displays an optical 2px gradient highlight to create gentle three-dimensional volume.
- Typography below the cover aligns left: title in `headline-sm`, author in `body-sm` (muted ink `#5A5650`), and price/format tag in `label-md` (`#1E352B`).

### Chips & Badges
- Non-pill, rectangular tags with 1px border (`#ECE4D6`) and subtle `#F5F0E8` background.
- Typography: `label-sm` in uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.1em`).
- Special editions (e.g., "First Edition", "Signed Copy") employ an Archival Gold accent border (`#C5A059`) with ink-toned text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature a crisp 1px border in `#ECE4D6` against a `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7` surface.
- Focus state: border turns to Deep Evergreen (`#1E352B`) without heavy glowing drop-shadows or color rings.
- Placeholder text in Pencil Umber (`#5A5650`), styled in `body-md` or `label-md`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Sharp square checkboxes (18px × 18px) with a 1px solid frame (`#191817`).
- Active state fills with Deep Evergreen (`#1E352B`) and reveals an off-white typographic checkmark.
- Radio buttons retain a crisp circular geometry with a centered interior solid pip.

### Lists & Shelves
- Horizontal scroll showcases ("The Reading Room", "Recent Colophons") incorporate visible left and right hairline boundaries with minimal brass arrow indicators.
- Vertical editorial lists feature a top border (`1px solid #ECE4D6`), with items spaced at `space-md` accompanied by roman numerals set in `EB Garamond`.
